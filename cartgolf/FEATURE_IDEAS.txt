Cart Golf - Feature Ideas
==========================

WORLDS & LEVELS
---------------
- 3 worlds (each is a different parking lot)
  * Could have different themes: suburban mall, city street, industrial area
  * Each world could have unique obstacles and layouts
  * Progressive difficulty across worlds

GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
------------------
- Slow time during re-flick (to allow microadjustment)?
  * Could add precision gameplay element
  * Maybe triggered by holding a key during aim
  * Visual effect like bullet-time with particles

POWERUPS & COLLECTIBLES
-----------------------
- Powerup: extra flick (pickup item)
  * Could spawn randomly on levels
  * Visual design: glowing shopping cart icon or +1 symbol
  * Adds strategic element - risk/reward for going off optimal path

FUTURE CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------
- Different cart types with unique physics?
- Multiplayer/co-op modes?
- Level editor for custom parking lots?
- Leaderboards and time trials?
- Weather effects (rain = slippery, wind = drift)?
